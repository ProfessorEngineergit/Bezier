The first beta of 1.0.2: numbers that take what you type, a centre rectangle that stays centred, and Space that starts a sketch.

**Typing values**
- A click into a number selects it, so typing 5 gives 5 and not 50. In the extrude field and in every bar of settings, the sketch plane's Offset, Tilt and Turn included.
- A digit typed while an extrude is offered goes into its distance, not to a shortcut.
- Changing the direction or the result after typing keeps the typed distance.
- An extrude of 0 builds nothing.

**Sketching**
- A centre rectangle keeps its centre when a side is typed: both sides grow.
- R opens the rectangle you used last, Center after Center.
- Select All in a sketch takes everything drawn.
- Space starts a sketch on the side the view squares to, also over empty space.
- In a flyout the names can be clicked, not only the icons.

**Parts**
- Join takes every part one extrude made, not only the first.

**Start page**
- The trailer plays beside the intro.

**Channels**
- Bezier has stable and beta. What reaches a server is in development builds only.

**Still true**
- The apps are not signed yet, so macOS and Windows ask before they open Bezier the first time. Every file is listed with its SHA-256 sum in the SHA256SUMS files of this release.
